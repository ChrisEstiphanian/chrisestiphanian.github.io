Nocturne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Nocturne is a puzzle game made by Blackcandle ("we"). This policy explains what information is used when you play Nocturne on Android, and the choices you have.

What the game itself keeps

Nocturne has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (levels, stars, light, restorations and settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it, and it is deleted if you uninstall the game. If you make a cathedral postcard, the picture is saved to your device's Pictures folder and is only shared if you choose to share it yourself.

Advertising

Nocturne is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, and to limit fraud, AdMob may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location it indicates,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (for example, viewing or tapping one).

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policy How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in a US state with its own privacy law, the game asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time from the Privacy button in the game's menu.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Security & privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling Nocturne deletes everything the game saved. We hold no data about you to delete.

Children

Nocturne is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
